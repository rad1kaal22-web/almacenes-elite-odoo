# Almacenes Elite Connector for Odoo

Buy office supplies, stationery and school material from **Almacenes Elite** (Spain) from your Odoo: shop on
almaceneselite.com already signed in (Purchase › Almacenes Elite), with your prices, approvals and payment method, and the
order comes back to Odoo as a confirmed purchase order. An RFQ of your Odoo (for instance, your replenishment) opens on the
website with its products in the cart; RFQs show stock, expected dates, alternatives with stock and how much is missing to
avoid shipping costs. Shipments, tracking numbers and returns follow on the purchase order, and Almacenes Elite invoices are
created as draft vendor bills with the original PDF.

* Branch `18.0`: Odoo 18 (Community and Enterprise, on Odoo.sh or on-premise). Other branches: `17.0`, `19.0`.
* New customers sign up from Odoo; existing customers link their account by tax ID, confirmed by email.

## Español

Compra material de oficina, papelería y escolar a **Almacenes Élite** desde tu Odoo: entras en almaceneselite.com ya
identificado (Compra › Almacenes Élite), con tus precios, tus aprobaciones y tu forma de pago, y el pedido vuelve a Odoo como
pedido de compra confirmado. Una solicitud de tu Odoo (por ejemplo, tu reposición) se abre en la cesta de la web; las
solicitudes dicen el stock, la fecha prevista, la alternativa con stock y cuánto falta para no pagar portes. El seguimiento y
las devoluciones van en el pedido de compra, y las facturas llegan como facturas de proveedor en borrador con el PDF original.

Soporte: pedidos@almaceneselite.com · https://almaceneselite.com
